# HarborFlow Operations

This context describes ship-agency port-call work and the reference knowledge used alongside it.

## Language

**Terminal Restriction**:
A recorded condition or permission for a specific service at a terminal, together with its source and verification date.
_Avoid_: Terminal contact, port restriction

**Terminal Contact**:
A person or department contacted for terminal-related coordination, with their role and contact details.
_Avoid_: Terminal restriction

**Knowledge Base Administrator**:
A person responsible for assigning access to terminal reference information and contacts, without authority over crew records.
_Avoid_: Crew administrator, system owner

**Terminal Restriction Editor**:
A person permitted to change terminal service conditions and their supporting details.
_Avoid_: Contact editor

**Terminal Contact Viewer**:
A named person permitted to see terminal contact records.
_Avoid_: Public viewer

**Terminal Contact Editor**:
A named person permitted to see and change terminal contact records.
_Avoid_: Restriction editor

**Knowledge Import Proposal**:
A privately submitted set of existing terminal restrictions or contacts, kept separate by type, whose individual entries await the owner's review before entering the shared Knowledge base.
_Avoid_: Published record, automatic import

**Knowledge Revision**:
An attributable record of a change to the shared terminal restrictions or contacts, including the prior and resulting content.
_Avoid_: Untracked edit
